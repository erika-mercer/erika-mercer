Is Erika a real person?+

Erika is a fictional AI-created character. This is an independent creative community project around her visual world.

Is the token live?+

No contract address or launch date has been published on this site yet. The token section will update when those details are provided.

Where will the contract appear?+

In the contract panel above. The complete address and a matching Pump.fun link will be added together. There is no active purchase link until then.

Can I make and save my own scene?+

Yes. Open Studio, choose a character, outfit and scene, then press Save your card. You can also share a link that restores your scene.

No. This is a community memecoin concept, with no promised returns or published investment terms. Memecoins are highly speculative.
